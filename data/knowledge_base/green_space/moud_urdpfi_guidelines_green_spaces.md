# Urban and Regional Development Plans Formulation and Implementation (URDPFI) Guidelines — Open and Green Space Norms

**Author:** Town and Country Planning Organisation (TCPO), Ministry of Urban Development (MoUD), Government of India  
**Publication Year:** 2015  
**Scope:** Planning Standards for Open Spaces, Recreational Areas, and Urban Green Coverage  
**Official Reference:** URDPFI Guidelines Vol. 1, Chapter 8  

## 1. National Per Capita Green Space Norms
The URDPFI guidelines mandate a benchmark of 10 to 12 square meters of organized public open and green space per capita in Indian urban areas. In dense metropolitan cities and municipal corporations where spatial expansion is constrained, a minimum standard of 8 to 10 square meters per person must be safeguarded within master plans and development control regulations (DCR).

## 2. Hierarchy of Urban Open Spaces
To ensure spatial equity and walkable access, urban green infrastructure is organized into four tiers:
1. **Housing Area Parks:** Minimum 0.5 hectares per 5,000 population; walking radius < 400 meters.
2. **Neighborhood Parks:** 1.0 to 2.0 hectares per 15,000 to 20,000 population; walking radius < 800 meters.
3. **Community Parks:** 5.0 to 10.0 hectares per 100,000 population; serving multi-ward catchments.
4. **District and City Parks / Urban Forests:** Exceeding 10.0 hectares; preserving ecological buffers, water catchment areas, and biodiversity corridors.

## 3. Green Coverage and Spatial Protection
- Master plans must zone open spaces under non-negotiable reservation to prevent diversion for infrastructure or commercial development.
- Green buffers of minimum 30 meters along natural watercourses (rivers, nullahs) and lake perimeters.
- Highway and arterial road corridor greening: multi-layer avenue plantation comprising native shade trees, mid-level flowering shrubs, and ground-level vegetative cover to trap road dust and mitigate urban heat island (UHI) effects.

## 4. Intervention Strategies for Space-Constrained Cities
Where vacant land is limited:
- Reclaim underutilized public utility buffer lands and decommissioned transport yards for pocket parks (mini-gardens).
- Encourage Miyawaki urban micro-forests on school, institutional, and municipal corporation premises to achieve dense green biomass rapidly.
- Incorporate mandatory rooftop greening and permeable paved surfaces in large institutional and commercial redevelopments.

## 5. Decision Support Metrics
Planners must evaluate:
- Green Space Coverage Ratio (%) = (Total Mapped Green Area / Total Municipal Administrative Area) * 100.
- Per Capita Green Space (sq.m/person) = Total Green Area / Census Resident Population.
- Percentage of urban residential parcels situated within a 10-minute (800m) walking radius of a publicly accessible park.
